## Terms defined on the page
Expense ratio: A fee payable to a mutual fund house for managing your mutual fund investments. It is the total percentage of a company's fund assets used for administrative, management, advertising, and other expenses.
Exit load: A fee payable to a mutual fund house for exiting a fund (fully or partially) before the completion of a specified period from the date of investment.
